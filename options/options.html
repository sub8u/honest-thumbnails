Summaries

Optional. A one-line gist and the key points of a video, so you can decide before you click.

Where you’ll see them
  • In your feed: hover a title card and press ✨ Summarize.
  • On a video page: a Summarize button under the player.
  • No AI needed: videos with chapters get a 📑 Chapters view either way.

Muted words

Videos whose title or channel name contains one of these are hidden from your feeds. Whole words only, not case-sensitive. Saved as you type.

“crypto” hides Crypto news but not Cryptography explained.

Trusted channels

These channels keep their original thumbnails and titles. To add one, hover any of its thumbnails on YouTube and press ⊘ Skip channel.
