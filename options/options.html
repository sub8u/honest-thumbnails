Summaries

With the title card on, hover a video and press ✨ Summarize for a gist and key points. Nothing leaves your device unless you choose your own API. Results are cached locally.

Used today0
Good to know
  • Saving asks Chrome for permission to contact only the endpoint you entered.
  • Ollama users: start it with OLLAMA_ORIGINS=chrome-extension://* so the extension is allowed to call it.
  • A summary uses the video’s captions when they can be read, otherwise its description. The card says which.
  • A small model typically costs well under a cent per video. Your provider’s pricing page has the exact rates.

Muted words

Videos whose title or channel name contains one of these are hidden from your feeds. Whole words only, not case-sensitive. Saved as you type.

“crypto” hides Crypto news but not Cryptography explained.

Trusted channels

These channels keep their original thumbnails and titles. To add one, hover any of its thumbnails on YouTube and press ⊘ Skip channel.
