# Changelog

## 1.5.0 (first public release)
- New look: *Honest* wordmark, compact popup, redesigned settings page with sections and an About page.
- Title cards tinted per channel; frosted hover toolbar; refreshed summary panel on video pages.
- New: 👁 Peek at the original thumbnail (hold Alt), dim watched videos, muted words, daily counts in the popup.
- Muted words and trusted channels now live in Settings.
- Fix: long summaries no longer push the card's heading out of view.
- Fix: summary cards show whole bullets that fit (plus "+N more") instead of chopping every line mid-word.
- New icon: an italic *h* on green, replacing the play-button icon that resembled YouTube's.
- On-device summaries show model download progress, request structured output, and check language support first.
- Faster on big feeds: page work is batched and yields to the browser, with no long main-thread tasks.
- Privacy policy rewritten section by section; `CHROMEWEBSTORE.md` added for the store dashboard.
- Fix: "ranks among the world's top 10" is no longer mistaken for a 10-item list; old summaries built that way are dropped.
